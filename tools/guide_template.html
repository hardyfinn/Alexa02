ALEXA02

Six drum voices from the open-source LXR engine, played from jacks, with two kits you can morph between. A VCV Rack module by Haer.

Alexa02 runs the actual sound engine from the Erica Synths LXR-02 firmware, which Erica released as open source in October 2026. The sequencer is gone. In its place every voice has its own trigger, accent, V/Oct and morph jacks, so you play it from whatever sequencer you like in Rack. The engine code is the firmware's own, so kits from the hardware (.snd files) load and sound the same.

Alexa02 is an unofficial port. It is not made or supported by Erica Synths. The engine code is GPL-3.0, and so is the module.

Install

  1. Copy Alexa02-2.0.0-mac-arm64.vcvplugin into your Rack plugins folder: ~/Library/Application Support/Rack2/plugins-mac-arm64/.
  2. Restart Rack. Alexa02 is under the brand Haer.

First sound

  1. Add Alexa02 and any clock and sequencer, for example a clock into a trigger sequencer.
  2. Patch a sequencer gate into TRIG under DRUM 1. That's your kick.
  3. Patch MIX L and MIX R to your audio interface.
  4. No sequencer to hand? Press PLAY. It fires whichever voice is selected.

A fresh module starts with the firmware's own init kit in A and in B. Right-click the panel and choose Load factory kit into A or into B to start from something more characterful.

Panel tour

The six voices

Each column is one voice: three drums (DRUM 1 to 3), SNARE, CYMBAL and HAT. Their engines differ:

VoiceWhat it is
DRUM 1, 2, 3An oscillator with a pitch sweep, a second oscillator for FM or mixing, a click layer and a filter. Kicks, toms, zaps, bleeps.
SNAREAn oscillator plus filtered noise, with a pitch sweep. The REPEAT setting turns it into a clap.
CYMBALThree oscillators frequency-modulating each other. Metallic rides, crashes and bells.
HATThree FM oscillators again, tuned for hats, with separate closed and open decays.
RowWhat it does
SELECTPicks the voice the edit knobs work on. It lights green when selected and flashes red when the voice plays.
TRIGPlays the voice on a rising edge. The HAT column's jack is the closed hat, and the jack in the OPEN box is the open hat. They choke each other, as on the hardware.
ACCAccent, read at the moment of the trigger. Unpatched or 0 V gives the hardware's normal level, and 5 V gives full accent. Turn on ACC>VOL on the AMP page if you want accent to change the volume. Accent is also a mod source (see Mod matrix).
V/OCTPitch, 1 V per octave. When patched, the jack sets the pitch and TUNE becomes a transpose around it: with TUNE at 0, 0 V plays middle C. Unpatched, TUNE alone sets the pitch. Unlike the hardware, the pitch isn't rounded to semitones, so slides and microtones work.
MORPHAdds to the main MORPH position, for this voice only. +10 V moves it all the way from A to B. See Morphing.
OUTThe voice on its own. Patching it takes that voice out of the MIX outputs, so you can process it separately without hearing it twice.

Editing

The screen shows the selected voice, the page and which kit you're editing. Under it are six values that match the six knobs below. Each value has a bar, and a thin mark on the bar shows where the same setting sits in the other kit, so you can see at a glance what will move when you morph.

The bottom line of the screen shows both kit names and the morph position, with a dot for each voice where its own MORPH jack has moved it.

Hover over a knob to see the full name of what it controls and its value, as with any Rack knob.

The pages

PageKnobs
OSCTUNE, FINE, WAVE (sine, triangle, saw, square, noise, PWM), PWM. The snare adds NOISE (colour) and MIX (tone against noise).
AMPATTACK, DECAY, SLOPE (decay shape), ACC>VOL. The snare and cymbal add REPEAT; the hat has separate CLOSED and OPEN decays.
PITCHDECAY, SLOPE and AMOUNT of the pitch sweep. Drums and snare only.
FMDrums: AMOUNT, RATIO, WAVE of the modulator, and MODE (FM or plain mix). Cymbal and hat: TUNE, FM amount and WAVE of oscillators 2 and 3.
CLICKA short sampled transient on top of the voice: TYPE (snap, click, tick, kick, rim, clap and more), LEVEL, PITCH.
FILTERCUTOFF, RESO, TYPE (LP, HP, BP, notch, peak and others), DRIVE.
LFORATE, SYNC, DEPTH, WAVE, RESET (which voice restarts it), PHASE. Each voice has one LFO. It only does something once a MOD slot uses it.
MIXLEVEL, PAN, S.RATE (sample-rate reduction), DRIVE, ROUTE (to the mix or through the FX).
FXOne effect for the whole kit: off, drive, ring mod, compressor or delay. Part 1 has TYPE, WET and four settings for that effect. Part 2 has the effect's shape, ratio or mode. A voice goes through the FX only when its ROUTE is set to FX.
MOD18 modulation slots, two per part, nine parts. See below.

Morphing

Kit A and kit B are two complete sets of settings. The MORPH knob blends every setting from A to B, all at once, as the LXR-02 does. Halfway gives you settings halfway between the two kits. Settings that are a list of choices, like a wave or a filter type, step through the choices in between on the way, as on the hardware.

The per-voice MORPH jacks add to the knob for one voice only. Two ways to use this:

  • Patch a slow LFO into DRUM 1's MORPH to make only the kick drift between its A and B versions.
  • Patch a random voltage, sampled on each hit, into the HAT's MORPH so every hat is slightly different.

A few things don't morph, as on the hardware: the mod matrix, the voice routing and the FX type.

A quick way to get a B kit

Load a kit into A, then right-click and choose Mutate all voices into B, with A little, Some or A lot. Now the MORPH knob sweeps from your kit into a variation of it. For one voice only, select it and press MUTATE.

Kits

The right-click menu has six factory kits plus the firmware's init kit. Load any of them into A or B.

KitCharacter
BoomLong sine kick and soft toms.
PunchShort kick with a click and drive, and a crisp snare.
ElectroFM toms, a clap and metallic square-wave hats.
MetalFM clang and crushed noise, with a ring mod on drum 2 and the cymbal.
DubSoft kick, rim snare and tom into a ping-pong delay.
CrushLow sample rate, driven filters and the whole kit through a compressor.

Good pairs to morph between: Boom into Punch, Electro into Metal, Dub into Crush.

Use Load kit file (.snd) to open kits saved on an LXR-02's card, and Save kit A/B as .snd to write them back. Kits are also saved with your patch.

Mod matrix

The MOD page has 18 slots. Each one has a source (SRC), a destination (DEST) and an amount (AMT, which can be negative).

  • Sources: MOD CV 1 to 5, each voice's LFO, and each voice's accent.
  • Destinations: nearly any setting of any voice, the FX settings, and MORPH itself.

Some examples:

  • CV 1 to Dr1 PITCH AMOUNT gives a kick whose sweep follows a sequencer lane.
  • Acc Snr to Snr FILTER CUTOFF makes accented snares brighter.
  • LFO Hat to Hat AMP CLOSED makes the hat decay breathe.
  • CV 2 to MORPH gives the matrix control of the kit morph. While a slot targets MORPH, it takes over from the knob.

Starter patches

Four on the floor, then morph

  1. Clock at 124 BPM. Send quarters to DRUM 1 TRIG, beats 2 and 4 to SNARE TRIG, and off-beat 8ths to HAT TRIG.
  2. Load Boom into A and Punch into B.
  3. Patch an LFO at 1/16 bars, 0 to 10 V, into the main MORPH CV and turn the CV trimmer fully right.

The groove travels between a round 808-style kit and a hard, clicky one over 16 bars.

A melodic tom line

  1. Load Electro into A.
  2. Patch a sequencer's gate into DRUM 3 TRIG and its pitch into DRUM 3 V/OCT.
  3. Patch DRUM 3 OUT through a reverb on its own.

Accents that change the sound

  1. Patch an accent gate from your sequencer into SNARE ACC.
  2. On the MOD page, set slot 1 to SRC Acc Snr, DEST Snr FILTER CUTOFF, and AMT +30.
  3. On the snare's AMP page, set ACC>VOL to on.

Good to know

  • The engine runs at the hardware's rate of 44.14 kHz, in blocks of 64 samples. A trigger plays at the start of the next block, up to about 1.5 ms later, just like on the LXR-02.
  • The LFOs and accents work only through the mod matrix. That's how the Eurorack firmware this port comes from works.
  • Kits from the LXR desktop firmware load, but they lose their old LFO and velocity routings. Rebuild those in the MOD page.
  • Several Alexa02s in one patch are fine. They share one engine and swap their state each block, which costs a little CPU per extra module.
  • The source code is at github.com/hardyfinn/alexa02.
